1930 Ford Panel Woodie was hand built in 2014 by noted wood master, Frank Wheeler.  I purchased it from Frank in 2024. The car is powered by a fuel injected 4.3 Vortec V6 coupled to a GM 4L60E tranny.  It has a Heidtz front end and a 9 inch Ford in the rear.  Creature comforts include AC and power windows.  The cowl and the front fenders are original Model A, while all other body parts are after market steel.
