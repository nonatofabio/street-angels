The Street Angels are Heritage members (original-era members), Legacy members (sons and grandsons of original members) and their close friends who share the hot rod bug.

The easiest way in is to show up. Come to **Hot Rods & Coffee** on the first Sunday of the month, or the **Monday lunch at Carr's**, and find a member. We meet monthly as a club.

## Get in touch

- Join the conversation in the [Street Angels Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2126708234222302)
- Email the club (address to be added)

## Add your car to the site

Members: send a photo and a few lines about the car (year, make, engine, trans, rear end, and the story) and it goes on the [Member's Cars](../cars/) page. Corrections welcome too.
