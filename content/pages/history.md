## 1952: a hundred hot rodders

The Santa Rosa Street Angels is a car club established in 1952. Original membership claimed approximately 100 members. The first six founding members were Ed Binggeli, Bob Consani, Frank Johnson, Aldino Dal Poggetto, Eldon Bryhan and Al Castelli. Their meeting place was Bings Speed Shop on Santa Rosa Avenue.

## 1956: the Santa Rosa Drag Strip

![1956 drag strip poster](poster.jpg "Drag strip poster, from the collection of Ed Dal Poggetto")

The City of Santa Rosa got the OK from the US Navy that the city could obtain a permit for the drag strip operation. The grand opening was April 19th, 1956. That first Sunday 2,400 spectators and 300 cars showed up to race. General admission was 90 cents, and participants paid another 75 cents to race. There were 33 classes for all types of cars, and a class for motorcycles.

Dick Berndt was president of the Street Angels. The club had 60 members at the time and needed a small army of people to run the strip, so Dick started the RETA, the Redwood Empire Timing Association. RETA had 25 car clubs from Northern California and 600 members in total. Its president was Ray Alpeter, also a Street Angel, and the first flag man to start the races. The Half Moon Bay, Cotati and Vacaville strips later became Tri County Drag Strip Inc.

![The RETA patch](street-angels-patch-002.png "The small Street Angel patch with RETA on the bottom, drawn by Aldino Dal Poggetto. From the collection of Ed Dal Poggetto.")

The small Street Angel patch with RETA on the bottom was drawn up by Aldino Dal Poggetto, and Dick Berndt had them made. They were worn on the back of a lightweight jacket by any Street Angel working the drag strip. The Petaluma Pacers, Devils Darlins, Dualers, Millwinders and Coachmen were among the RETA clubs that helped run the strip.

## 1956: the first car show in Santa Rosa

![Rose Festival Auto Show program](cover.jpg "The official program of the 1956 Rose Festival Auto Show")

![Inside the program](img_0001-images-1.jpg "Inside the 1956 program")

The Rose Festival Auto Show ran May 16th to 20th, 1956. It was the first hot rod and custom car show hosted by the Santa Rosa Street Angels, and the first car show ever held in Santa Rosa. It was co-sponsored by the Santa Rosa Junior Chamber of Commerce. The mastermind behind it was Dick Berndt.

## 2015: the club comes back

![Street Angels today](243374993_10227832891632299_8990588031352247940_n.jpg "The club today")

The club resurfaced in 2015 after a temporary hiatus, with a core group of 30-plus members: Heritage members, Legacy members (sons and grandsons of original members) and their close friends. Activities include monthly meetings, the first-Sunday Hot Rods & Coffee, the Monday hot rodders' lunch at Carr's that the club originated, and other hot rod events around the county.

More history and period photos are on the club's [Kustomrama page](https://kustomrama.com/wiki/Street_Angels_of_Santa_Rosa).
